HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

Empowering the Chief Human Officer (Keshav) for live UX friction audits and reality testing.
📱
1. The Smoke Test
  • Keshav monitors the #bolt-github-builds channel for live deployment links.
  • Immediately opens the app on a physical mobile device.
  • Conducts a rapid 30-second friction test to identify fatal UI/UX crashes.
👁️
2. Reality Audit
  • Evaluates the application specifically from a "Non-Tech Person" perspective.
  • Tests buttons, navigation flow, and screen responsiveness.
  • Verifies that AI-generated copywriting sounds natural and relatable.
📢
3. Feedback Loop
  • Takes screenshots of any broken, overlapping, or confusing elements.
  • Posts visual evidence and brief critique directly into #keshav-smoke-tests.
  • CEO processes the feedback into new Kanban tickets for the AI engineers.
